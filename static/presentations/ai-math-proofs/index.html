Tech News · two AI-made proofs in one week

Machine-checked, not yet understood

On Nov 20, Levent Alpöge and Claude released a Lean proof of the Riemann Hypothesis. On Nov 23, a paper written entirely by Claude claimed VP ≠ VNP. Both proofs compile in Lean, but they stand on very different footing.

RH article · Lean FRO statement · VP ≠ VNP paper · VP ≠ VNP Lean

Result 1 · the Riemann Hypothesis

Every nontrivial zero sits on one line

Claimed and formalized: RH, plus the Generalized RH for Dirichlet L-functions. Not claimed: the extended or grand RH. Posed in 1859; a Clay Millennium Prize problem.

Result 1 · how the proof goes

A function-field trick, carried over to the integers

The idea

Stepanov gave a short proof of RH for curves over finite fields using a "multiplicity" trick. Over the integers there's no multiplicity, which Alpöge likens to "differentiating whole numbers". The proof replaces it with a chain of theta lifts, of length about √X for a prime sum of length X.

Step 1: a weaker estimate is enough

Bounding a smoothed prime-counting error only on scattered ranges, G(x) ≤ x1/2+ε for Y < x < Y3/2, already forces RH. This step uses classical Landau–Turán methods.

Step 2: prove that estimate

Repeated theta correspondence moves automorphic forms between unitary groups. Arakelov theory compares the resulting divisibility with the size of the periods. Tao: it uses "the full power of modern developments on the Langlands program".

The strategy comes from AI-agent summaries of the Lean source by Robin Zhang (IHES) and Michael Rubinstein (Waterloo), plus Alpöge's 4-page remark. No complete human-readable proof exists yet. Nuccio: "it basically contains all number theory produced in the last 50 years."

Result 1 · verification

Checked every way the Lean community knows

17M lines

175,187 modules. Dozens of people rebuilt it. Sutherland's sandboxed rebuild reproduced the 61.6 GB proof export byte for byte.

Right statement

It proves Mathlib's own, expert-vetted RiemannHypothesis. Two independent tools (comparator, con-leche) confirm the theorem matches that statement.

No extra axioms

Only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. No sorry, native_decide or kernel bypasses in any file.

Many kernels

Lean's kernel (48 h), its paranoid mode (72 h), nanoda (Rust), lean4lean, and con-leche/con-ron, which are formally proved never to accept a false proof.

Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative, Nov 21: "We have full confidence that Alpöge's proof of the Riemann Hypothesis is correct." Independent dashboards: Sutherland (MIT), whose checks were run with Claude Code, and Rubinstein (Waterloo).

Result 2 · background

VP vs VNP: determinant vs permanent

VP: polynomial families with polynomial-size arithmetic circuits (only +, × and constants). VNP: exponential-size sums of VP polynomials. VP ≠ VNP is the algebraic analogue of P ≠ NP.

Result 2 · the claim

A superpolynomial lower bound, over every field

-- vp-vnp/Statement.lean (the headline bound)
def Beyond (c : ℝ) (m s : ℕ) : Prop :=
  (m : ℝ) ^ (c * Real.logb 2 m ^ (1 / 3 : ℝ)) < s
-- every circuit for perm m (char ≠ 2) or the Hamiltonian cycle polynomial
-- has s gates with Beyond c m s, for some c > 0 and all m ≥ m₀

Claims

More than mc·(log₂ m)1/3 gates, for approximating circuits too. So VP ⊊ VNP, and VNP isn't even in the closure of VP, over every field.

Doesn't claim

P ≠ NP. Anything about Boolean circuits, since algebraic circuits must work on all field inputs, not just 0/1. An exponential bound. An explicit m₀ or the best exponent.

Who checked

Paper and Lean written by AI models. "No human mathematician has checked the proof." The repo's CI runs comparator and the nanoda kernel.

Valiant posed the question in 1979. Past lower bounds needed restrictions such as bounded depth; this claims to rule out every polynomial-size circuit. Submitted to alphaXiv's AI track on 2026-11-23, with "Claude" as sole author.

Side by side

Lean checks the proof, not what you meant

Rows ①–④ are the Lean community's four criteria. Why ① matters: the Xena blog's example ∀ a b c n : ℕ, n ≥ 3 → aⁿ + bⁿ ≠ cⁿ compiles fine but is false, because ℕ includes 0. VP, VNP and "circuit" aren't in Mathlib, so the repo defines them itself.

Reactions

Historic, if it holds; awkward either way

"A historic milestone"

James Maynard, if it fully checks out. Clay president Martin Bridson: "an announcement of historic significance", but understanding will take time.

Skeptics

Granville criticized the 4-page sketch and its timing, a day after Anthropic's IPO. Radziwiłł noted there's no human understanding in the formalization. Alpöge himself: "The Lean is full of garbage obviously, but compiles."

A human rival

Hours later, Ravi Vakil posted Alessandro Masullo's "all human, with no AI" RH preprint as a "hurried announcement" (Nature). It's unverified.

Who gets credit?

The Leiden Declaration, signed by thousands, says credit and responsibility belong to humans. The WSJ called it "centaur science". The VP ≠ VNP paper lists only Claude.

Discussion

Does any of this touch Bitcoin?

RH: no

secp256k1 rests on the elliptic-curve discrete-log problem, and SHA-256 on its own design. Neither depends on RH. GRH makes the Miller primality test deterministic, but AKS (2002) already did that unconditionally.

VP ≠ VNP: no

It's a worst-case bound for algebraic circuits. Cryptography needs average-case hardness for Boolean computation. It neither proves nor breaks any assumption Bitcoin uses.

  • If no human understands a proof but six independent kernels accept it, is it a proof? What would change your mind?
  • Lean shrinks the trust problem to "is the statement right?" Who should review statements, and how?
  • Could the same pipeline (AI writes it, Lean checks it) verify consensus code or Miniscript compilers?
  • Should an AI be listed as an author, as on the VP ≠ VNP paper?
